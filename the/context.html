Mid-2021, I was 14.

I went on
summer vacation to
Charlevoix. We stood at
Chalet Mirador for a few days.

I remember sitting in the RV looking out the window admiring everything, filming alot of it.

I had recently discovered Lil Peep,
and with him, The Microphones.

I brought my dad's electric guitar with no amp.

I remember playing Headless Horseman
many nights alone in my room and living room.

My room was on the
second story with three beds.
My bed was by the window.

The mattresses, and especially pillows, were blissfully comfortable and tepid.

The trees were rustling in the breeze as I fell asleep. It sounded like waves by the ocean.

When my mom found out, she got angry, worried I'd get myself sick. But I'd die to miss it.

That vacation forever
changed my life's path.

Before, I was lost,
not knowing what I liked,
what I wanted to look
forward to in life.

That vacation, I found myself.
