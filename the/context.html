Mid-2021, I was 14.

I went on summer vacation to Charlevoix with the neighbor's RV. We stood at Chalet Mirador for a few days.

I remember sitting in the RV looking out the window admiring everything, filming alot of it.

I had recently discovered Lil Peep, and with him The Microphones.

I brought my dad's electric guitar (no amp). Many nights in my room and in the living room, I remember playing Headless Horseman alone.

I felt meaning.

I slept in a room on the second story with three beds: one twin by the window, another by the wall, and a double in the center.

The beds, and especially pillows, were blissfully comfortable and tepid.

I remember listening to the trees rustling in the breeze creating natural white noise, like waves by the ocean.

My mom would get angry every time, worried I'd get sick. Well I'd die to miss it.

This vacation, I believe, forever changed the path I'd take.

Before it, I was lost, not knowing what I liked, who I wanted to be, what I wanted to look forward to in life.

With it, I found out.
